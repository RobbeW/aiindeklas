# H1 decision — Source inventory exceptions

- Status: approved
- Approved: 2026-07-16
- Approver: Robbe Wulgaert

## Decision

Proceed with the migration. Photography and videography paths are dated and no
longer reflect the owner's work or the focus of this migration. Those eleven
routes are explicitly excluded as out of scope and remain traceable in the URL
ledger.

The remaining recommendations are also approved:

- redirect the unavailable `/contact-english` route to `/contactinfo`;
- derive the two rate-limited tag archives from migrated article metadata;
- exclude inaccessible `/info` without inventing replacement copy;
- exclude the failed noncanonical pagination query because successful adjacent
  pagination and RSS records account for its article content.

No remote repository, registrar, or DNS action is authorised by this decision.
