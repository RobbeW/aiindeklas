## Hi, I am Robbe.

## Nice to meet you!

I am a programming, Artificial Intelligence and Design Thinking teacher in Ghent. You can usually find me in my classroom as a full-time teacher at Sint-Lievenscollege or as an external assistant at the University of Ghent. My work focuses on education, programming, artificial intelligence and Design Thinking.

## Education

Since 2016 I have been working as a programming teacher in Ghent. There I combine my knowledge and motivation to constantly develop new projects for my students. Online lesson packages JavaScript or Python? Check! Lessons about artificial intelligence (AI) in secondary school? Check! Latin lessons in Minecraft or product design in virtual reality? Check and double check!

The latter project was even picked up by the King Baudouin Foundation and the National Lottery. But judge for yourself!
